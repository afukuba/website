Jul 2021 → Current
Mixed Volumes of Normal Complexes
Collaborative project with Lauren Nowak and Dusty Ross
  • Developed a theory of mixed volumes on truncations of tropical fans, called normal complexes, and extended the relationship between their (mixed) volume and (mixed) degrees of divisors of the corresponding Chow ring.
  • Used the theory of mixed volumes of normal complexes to provide a volume‑theoretic proof of the Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture on the log‑concavity of characteristic polynomials of matroids.

≈ Manifesto

I believe in Federico Ardila’s axioms from Todos Cuentan:

01

Mathematical potential is distributed equally among different groups, irrespective of geographic, demographic, and economic boundaries.

02

Everyone can have joyful, meaningful, and empowering mathematical experiences.

03

Mathematics is a powerful, malleable tool that can be shaped and used differently by various communities to serve their needs.

04

Every student deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
